PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy and requests

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you request a roof assessment, we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number, property ZIP code, roof material, and any message you choose to include.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, schedule and carry out your roof assessment, and follow up about it. We do not sell your information.

How it is delivered

Form submissions are sent to SolHydrix by email through a third-party form delivery service (FormSubmit). That service processes the submission only to deliver it to us.

Financing application

The financing PDF is filled out on your own device. This website does not collect or upload the information you enter into it. Please keep financial information out of the assessment form.

Your choices

To ask us to update or delete the information you sent, reply to any email from SolHydrix or submit a request through the assessment form with a note.
